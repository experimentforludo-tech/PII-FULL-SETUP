How to Find Your Passphrase

Follow these steps to locate your 24‑word passphrase in Pi Wallet

  1. Open the Pi Browser app on your phone.

  2. Tap on the Wallet icon (usually at the bottom).

  3. If you already have a wallet, unlock it with your passcode or biometric.

  4. Inside the wallet, look for Settings or Security (often a gear icon or three‑dots menu).

  5. Find the option labelled "Reveal Passphrase" or "Backup Passphrase".

  6. You may need to re‑enter your passcode or confirm your identity.

  7. The 24 words will appear on screen. Write them down in the exact order shown.

  8. Keep this phrase safe and secure.

Note: If you haven't created a wallet yet, you will need to create one first. Your passphrase will be shown during the creation process.

⚠️ Important: Never share your passphrase with anyone. Pi Network will never ask for your passphrase. Keep it offline and secure.
