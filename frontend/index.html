Frequently Asked Questions

What is Pi Unlocker?

Pi Unlocker is a tool for checking your Pi wallet balance and initiating the unlock process for eligible Pi.

How long does the process take?

Eligible requests may be processed within up to 24 hours. Actual processing time can vary.

What do I need to check my balance?

You will need your 24-word wallet passphrase to verify wallet ownership and check your balance.

Will my mining speed be affected?

Yes, unlocking your locked Pi will reduce your mining speed. This is part of the unlocking process.

How can I re-store my mining speed?

To re-store your mining speed, you must add at least 3 people to your group. This will restore your mining rate.

What percentage will I receive?

You will receive 88% of your total locked Pi balance. The remaining 12% is deducted as swap charges.

Is my information secure?

We use encrypted connections and secure storage to protect your wallet information.
